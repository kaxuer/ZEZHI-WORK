Our Story

A Legacy Gathered Along the Way

Our family's collecting story began with the journeys of earlier generations. My forebears traveled between nearby towns and counties, making their living through trade. Their travels brought them into contact with the character of different places; when an object spoke to them, they chose to keep it. In time, those early keepsakes became the first traces of our family's collecting story.

My father carried this life on the road into a new generation, traveling between provinces for business. Along the way, he too took an interest in the objects he encountered, gradually adding pieces of his own to the collection. Each one recalls something of his journeys, observations, and years spent far from home.

In the mid-to-late 1990s, I set out on my own collecting journey. What began with the experiences of those before me became a personal pursuit that continues to this day. The journeys of my forebears, my father's encounters, and my own searching now meet in a family collection shaped over time. I hope to honor that inheritance by continuing to seek, cherish, and share these objects—and the memories they carry.
